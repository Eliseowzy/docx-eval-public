`cases/layout/families/nimbleoffice-fixtures/` and
`cases/layout/families/nimbleoffice-experiments/` come from the BetterOffice
repository (`crates/docx-edit/tests/fixtures` and `tools/word-parity/experiments`),
licensed under the Apache License 2.0. The project has since been renamed to
NimbleOffice; the family directory names are kept unchanged for continuity. The two
table-split bases `01-continuation-clipping.docx` and
`02-page-bottom-boundary.docx` are fixtures from the same repository and are
byte-identical to `crates/docx-edit/tests/fixtures/table-split-bands/`.
Source: <https://github.com/openooxml/betteroffice>; the project was renamed, and the
current repository is <https://github.com/Eliseowzy/nimbleoffice>.
